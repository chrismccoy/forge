# Accessibility facts
Covers: WCAG 2.2 AA, WAI-ARIA, automated testing (axe-core, Lighthouse, Pa11y)
Verified: 2026-09-29 against official documentation. Re-verify facts older than 12 months.

## Automated testing

### A11Y-01 A clean axe/Lighthouse run is not WCAG conformance
- Trap: "CI runs axe (or Lighthouse scores 100), so we meet WCAG 2.2 AA."
- Reality: Deque says axe-core finds on average 57% of WCAG issues; uncertain checks go to `incomplete` for manual review. No axe rule covers 2.4.7 Focus Visible, meaningful sequence, caption quality or keyboard operation. Lighthouse scores a weighted subset of axe audits; manual audits do not count.
- Detect: "axe passes" or "Lighthouse 100" as the acceptance bar; no keyboard or screen-reader test plan.
- Fix: Keep axe as a regression gate; add manual keyboard, zoom and screen-reader checks.
- Source: axe-core README - https://github.com/dequelabs/axe-core

### A11Y-02 axe tags match exactly; WCAG 2.2 has one rule
- Trap: `runOnly: ['wcag22aa']` runs every WCAG 2.2 AA check.
- Reality: Tags are `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21a`, `wcag21aa`, `wcag22aa`, plus `best-practice` (not WCAG) and `experimental`. `runOnly` matches tags exactly, so `wcag22aa` alone skips all 2.0/2.1 rules. The only `wcag22aa` rule is `target-size`, disabled by default. Pa11y defaults to HTML_CodeSniffer, WCAG2AA, with no 2.2 option.
- Detect: a lone `wcag22aa` tag; claims that tools check 2.4.11, 2.5.7 or 3.3.8.
- Fix: List every level tag, enable `target-size`, test the other 2.2 criteria by hand.
- Source: axe-core API.md - https://github.com/dequelabs/axe-core/blob/develop/doc/API.md

### A11Y-03 Overlays do not make a site conformant
- Trap: "An accessibility widget/overlay makes the site WCAG compliant."
- Reality: The FTC fined accessiBe $1M (2025) and barred claims that its automated tool makes any website WCAG-compliant; the claims were false or unsubstantiated.
- Detect: a third-party overlay script as the compliance plan.
- Fix: Fix the markup and components; never budget an overlay as remediation.
- Source: FTC press release - https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/ftc-order-requires-online-marketer-pay-1-million-deceptive-claims-its-ai-product-could-make-websites

## ARIA and live regions

### A11Y-04 role=alert is not for content present at load
- Trap: A server-rendered banner gets `role="alert"`/`aria-live="assertive"`, and tests assert that role on static content.
- Reality: Live regions exist to announce dynamic changes. `alert` (implicit assertive, atomic) is for important, time-sensitive updates; MDN says not to use it on content the user has not interacted with. Announcement of pre-filled alerts varies by browser/AT; injecting a filled live region is often silent.
- Detect: `role="alert"` in page-load markup; live regions created together with their message.
- Fix: Render static notices as plain content or a labelled region. Keep an empty live region in the initial markup and change its text later.
- Source: MDN, ARIA alert role - https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Accessibility/ARIA/Reference/Roles/alert_role

### A11Y-05 Status messages need a role, not focus (4.1.3 AA)
- Trap: "Saved", "3 results" or "Added to cart" are visual only, or focus jumps to them.
- Reality: Status messages must be exposed so AT announces them without focus: `role="status"` (polite) for success/state, `alert` for errors, `log`, `progressbar`. Messages that move focus or load a page are out of scope.
- Detect: toasts, result counts, cart and autosave indicators with no role.
- Fix: Send them through a persistent `role="status"` region; keep `alert` for errors.
- Source: Understanding SC 4.1.3 - https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html

### A11Y-06 ARIA adds no behavior
- Trap: `<div role="button">` or a custom listbox is accessible because it has the role.
- Reality: Rules of ARIA use: prefer native elements; ARIA widgets must be made keyboard-operable by the author; never put `aria-hidden="true"` on focusable elements; every interactive element needs an accessible name. APG modal dialogs move focus in, trap Tab, close on Escape and return focus to the opener.
- Detect: div/span click handlers, icon-only buttons with no name, custom modals without focus handling.
- Fix: Use `<button>`, `<a href>`, `<dialog>`, `<select>`; follow the APG pattern for custom widgets.
- Source: Using ARIA - https://www.w3.org/TR/using-aria/

## WCAG 2.2 criteria

### A11Y-07 WCAG 2.2 adds six A/AA criteria and drops 4.1.1
- Trap: A "WCAG 2.2 AA" blueprint lists only 2.1 criteria, or still tests 4.1.1 Parsing.
- Reality: New AA: 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum), 2.5.7 Dragging Movements, 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum), 3.3.8 Accessible Authentication (Minimum). New A: 3.2.6 Consistent Help, 3.3.7 Redundant Entry. 4.1.1 Parsing is obsolete and removed.
- Detect: 2.2 claims with no review of sticky UI, drag, targets or login.
- Fix: Add these to the acceptance checklist; drop 4.1.1 checks.
- Source: What's New in WCAG 2.2 - https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/new-in-22/

### A11Y-08 Dragging needs a click/tap alternative (2.5.7)
- Trap: A drag-and-drop seat map or kanban is fine because it works by keyboard.
- Reality: Every drag action needs a single-pointer alternative unless dragging is essential. Keyboard support alone does not satisfy 2.5.7.
- Detect: "drag to assign/reorder/move", drag-only upload zones.
- Fix: Add tap-to-select then tap-to-place, move buttons or a menu, as well as keyboard support.
- Source: Understanding SC 2.5.7 - https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/dragging-movements.html

### A11Y-09 Targets 24x24 CSS px or spaced (2.5.8)
- Trap: Tiny icon buttons are fine, or 44 px is the AA rule.
- Reality: AA needs 24x24 CSS px, except: spacing (24 px circles on undersized targets do not overlap other targets), an equivalent control, inline links in text, user-agent-sized controls, essential. 44 px is 2.5.5 (AAA).
- Detect: dense toolbars, icon actions in table rows, small close icons.
- Fix: Size or space pointer targets to 24 px.
- Source: Understanding SC 2.5.8 - https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html

### A11Y-10 Sticky UI must not hide focus (2.4.11)
- Trap: Sticky headers, chat widgets and cookie bars can overlay content freely.
- Reality: A focused component must not be entirely hidden by author content; a non-modal cookie banner that covers it fails.
- Detect: fixed header/footer, persistent chat panel, bottom cookie bar.
- Fix: Use `scroll-padding`, make banners modal or push content; tab through to test.
- Source: Understanding SC 2.4.11 - https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html

## Forms and authentication

### A11Y-11 Login cannot demand a cognitive test (3.3.8)
- Trap: Puzzle or text CAPTCHA at login, paste blocked on password/OTP fields.
- Reality: Any auth step that requires recall, transcription or solving needs an alternative method or an assisting mechanism. Password managers and paste (including 2FA codes) must work. Object recognition and the user's own images are allowed at AA.
- Detect: paste blocking, OTP boxes that reject paste, custom CAPTCHA.
- Fix: Allow paste/autofill (`autocomplete="current-password"`, `one-time-code`); offer passkeys, email links or non-puzzle bot checks.
- Source: Understanding SC 3.3.8 - https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/accessible-authentication-minimum.html

### A11Y-12 Errors in text; don't re-ask data (3.3.1, 3.3.7)
- Trap: A red border marks the error; checkout re-asks the address for billing.
- Reality: 3.3.1 (A): a detected error is identified and described in text; color/icons only supplement. 3.3.7 (A): data entered earlier in the same process is prefilled or selectable; browser autofill does not count.
- Detect: color-only validation; repeated address or contact steps.
- Fix: Text errors linked via `aria-describedby`; "same as shipping" or prefill.
- Source: Understanding SC 3.3.7 - https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/redundant-entry.html

### A11Y-13 Session timeouts need warning and extension (2.2.1)
- Trap: The session silently expires after N minutes and loses form data.
- Reality: 2.2.1 (A) covers server session limits: let users turn off or adjust (10x) the limit, or warn with at least 20 seconds to extend by a simple action (at least 10 times), unless real-time or essential.
- Detect: fixed idle timeout with no pre-expiry dialog.
- Fix: Warn before expiry with an "extend" button; keep entered data.
- Source: Understanding SC 2.2.1 - https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/timing-adjustable.html

## Visual and non-text

### A11Y-14 Contrast covers controls and focus; outlines matter
- Trap: Contrast applies only to body text, and `outline: none` is fine.
- Reality: 1.4.3: 4.5:1 text, 3:1 large (18pt or 14pt bold). 1.4.11: 3:1 for component boundaries/states, focus indicators and meaningful graphics; disabled controls exempt. 2.4.7 (AA) needs visible focus; removing outlines with no replacement is failure F78. 1.4.10: reflow at 320 CSS px without 2-D scrolling (maps and tables excepted).
- Detect: pale borders or placeholders, `outline: 0`, fixed-width layouts.
- Fix: Put contrast pairs in design tokens, style `:focus-visible`, test at 400% zoom.
- Source: Understanding SC 1.4.11 - https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html

### A11Y-15 Canvas and visual maps are invisible to AT
- Trap: A canvas/SVG seat map, floor plan or chart needs no alternative.
- Reality: Canvas is a bitmap; drawn objects are not exposed to assistive technology. Visual-only information or actions fail 1.1.1 and keyboard/name requirements.
- Detect: `<canvas>`, WebGL or image maps as the only way to choose or read data.
- Fix: Provide an equivalent list or table view with the same actions, plus a text summary.
- Source: MDN, canvas - https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/canvas

### A11Y-16 Mobile text must scale; controls need labels
- Trap: Fixed font sizes are fine; icon buttons explain themselves.
- Reality: iOS Dynamic Type scales only text-style fonts (`preferredFont(forTextStyle:)`, SwiftUI `.body`); fixed-size fonts do not. Android scales `sp`, not `dp`. TalkBack reads `contentDescription`; VoiceOver reads the accessibility label.
- Detect: hard-coded pt/dp font sizes, unlabeled icon buttons.
- Fix: Use text styles/`sp`, let layouts grow, label every icon control.
- Source: Apple, Scaling fonts automatically - https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/scaling-fonts-automatically

## Legal scope

### A11Y-17 US public-sector rules require 2.1 AA; dates moved
- Trap: "ADA Title II requires WCAG 2.2 by April 2026," or Section 508 binds private businesses.
- Reality: DOJ's 2024 Title II rule requires WCAG 2.1 AA for state/local government web and mobile apps, including public schools and universities. An April 20, 2026 interim final rule moved compliance to April 26, 2027 (population 50,000+) and April 26, 2028 (smaller, special districts). HHS Section 504 (2.1 AA) moved to May 11, 2027 / May 10, 2028. Section 508 binds federal agencies and uses WCAG 2.0 AA.
- Detect: the old 2026 date, "2.2 is legally required", 508 cited for a private product.
- Fix: Design to 2.2 AA (it also meets 2.1 AA); cite the right law and date.
- Source: ADA.gov web rule - https://www.ada.gov/resources/2024-03-08-web-rule/

### A11Y-18 EAA applies since June 28, 2025
- Trap: EU accessibility law only affects public-sector sites.
- Reality: The European Accessibility Act covers e-commerce, banking, e-books, transport and telephony services placed on the market after June 28, 2025; micro-enterprise service providers (under 10 staff and EUR 2M turnover) are exempt. EN 301 549 v3.2.1 (WCAG 2.1 AA) remains the legal reference until v4.1.1 (WCAG 2.2, Sept 2026) is cited in the Official Journal.
- Detect: an EU consumer service with no accessibility requirement.
- Fix: Target WCAG 2.2 AA and document how the service conforms.
- Source: Your Europe, Accessibility - https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/accessibility/index_en.htm
